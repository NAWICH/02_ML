SECTION 1: GENERAL KNOWLEDGE (सामान्य ज्ञान)

NEPAL HISTORY
- Nepal was unified by Prithvi Narayan Shah in 1768 BS (1743 AD)
- The Shah dynasty ruled Nepal for over 240 years
- Nepal became a Federal Democratic Republic on 2065 BS Jestha 15 (May 28, 2008)
- The first Constituent Assembly election was held in 2064 BS (2007 AD)
- King Tribhuvan returned from exile in India in 2007 BS ending the Rana regime
- The Rana oligarchy ruled Nepal from 1846 to 1951
- The Sugauli Treaty was signed between Nepal and British India in 1816 AD
- Nepal joined the United Nations in 1955 AD

NEPAL GEOGRAPHY
- Nepal covers an area of 147,181 square kilometers
- Nepal is a landlocked country bordered by China in the north and India in the south, east, and west
- Nepal has 7 provinces, 77 districts, 753 local levels
- The highest peak in the world, Mount Everest (8848.86m), is in Nepal
- Nepal has 6000+ rivers and rivulets
- Major rivers: Koshi, Gandaki, Karnali
- Terai region covers about 17% of Nepal's total area
- Hilly region covers about 68% of Nepal's total area
- Himalayan region covers about 15% of Nepal's total area
- Nepal's capital city is Kathmandu

NEPAL CONSTITUTION 2072
- Nepal's Constitution 2072 (2015) is the seventh constitution
- The Constitution was promulgated on Ashwin 3, 2072 BS (September 20, 2015)
- Nepal is a Federal Democratic Republic with 3 tiers of government
- Three levels: Federal, Provincial, Local
- The President is the head of state
- The Prime Minister is the head of government
- There are 165 articles in the fundamental rights section
- The Supreme Court is the highest court
- Nepal has a bicameral parliament: National Assembly (upper) and House of Representatives (lower)
- House of Representatives has 275 members (165 FPTP + 110 PR)
- National Assembly has 59 members

GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE
- Federal Parliament consists of the National Assembly and the House of Representatives
- Provincial Parliament is unicameral in all 7 provinces
- Local level governments include Rural Municipalities, Municipalities, Sub-Metropolitan Cities, Metropolitan Cities
- Nepal has 6 Metropolitan Cities, 11 Sub-Metropolitan Cities, 276 Municipalities, 460 Rural Municipalities
- Kathmandu Metropolitan City is the largest city

NATIONAL SYMBOLS
- National flower: Rhododendron (Laligurans)
- National bird: Danphe (Himalayan Monal)
- National animal: Cow
- National color: Crimson red
- National language: Nepali
- National flag: Double pennant shape (world's only non-quadrilateral national flag)

SECTION 2: MATHEMATICS (गणित)

NUMBER SYSTEM
- Natural numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5...
- Whole numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5...
- Integers: ...-3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3...
- Rational numbers: numbers expressible as p/q where q≠0
- Irrational numbers: √2, π, e

BASIC FORMULAS
- Area of rectangle = length × breadth
- Area of circle = πr²
- Area of triangle = ½ × base × height
- Perimeter of rectangle = 2(length + breadth)
- Volume of cube = side³
- Volume of cuboid = length × breadth × height
- Pythagoras theorem: a² + b² = c²
- Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
- Compound Interest = P(1 + r/100)^n - P

PERCENTAGE AND RATIO
- If A is 20% more than B, then A = 1.2B
- If A is 20% less than B, then A = 0.8B
- Profit% = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100
- Loss% = (Loss / Cost Price) × 100
- Speed = Distance / Time

REASONING AND LOGIC
- If all A are B and all B are C, then all A are C (syllogism)
- Venn diagrams represent sets and relationships
- Blood relations: common types in exams
  Father's sister = Aunt (Phuphu)
  Mother's brother = Uncle (Mama)
  Father's brother = Uncle (Kaka)

SECTION 3: NEPALI LANGUAGE (नेपाली भाषा)

GRAMMAR BASICS
- Nepali has 11 vowels: अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ, ऋ
- Nepali has 33 consonants
- Karta (Subject) + Karma (Object) + Kriya (Verb) = Basic sentence structure
- Sandhi: joining of letters/words
  Swar Sandhi: joining of vowels
  Byanjan Sandhi: joining of consonants
  Bisarga Sandhi: joining with visarga

PARTS OF SPEECH (शब्दभेद)
- Noun (नाम): names of persons, places, things
- Pronoun (सर्वनाम): replaces noun (म, तिमी, ऊ)
- Verb (क्रिया): action words (खानु, पिउनु, सुत्नु)
- Adjective (विशेषण): describes noun (राम्रो, ठूलो, सानो)
- Adverb (क्रियाविशेषण): describes verb (बिस्तारै, छिटो, यहाँ)
- Preposition (परसर्ग): shows relationship (मा, बाट, को, ले)
- Conjunction (संयोजक): joins words/clauses (र, तर, किनकि)
- Interjection (विस्मयादिबोधक): expresses emotion (अहा!, ओहो!)

IMPORTANT LITERARY TERMS
- Alankara (अलंकार): figures of speech
- Upamaaa (उपमा): simile - "जस्तो/झैं" used
- Rupak (रूपक): metaphor - direct comparison
- Anupras (अनुप्रास): alliteration
- Yamak (यमक): same word different meanings

SAMPLE PRACTICE QUESTIONS

GK QUESTIONS:
Q1: What is the capital of Nepal?
A) Pokhara  B) Kathmandu  C) Biratnagar  D) Lalitpur
Answer: B) Kathmandu
Explanation: Kathmandu is the capital and largest city of Nepal, serving as the center of political, cultural, and commercial activities.

Q2: When was Nepal's Constitution 2072 promulgated?
A) 2072 Shrawan 1  B) 2072 Ashwin 3  C) 2072 Baisakh 1  D) 2072 Poush 15
Answer: B) 2072 Ashwin 3 (September 20, 2015)
Explanation: Nepal's current constitution, the seventh in Nepal's history, was promulgated on Ashwin 3, 2072 BS.

Q3: How many districts does Nepal have?
A) 72  B) 75  C) 77  D) 80
Answer: C) 77
Explanation: Nepal was reorganized into 77 districts from the previous 75 districts after the new federal structure.

Q4: What is Nepal's national flower?
A) Lotus  B) Rose  C) Rhododendron  D) Jasmine
Answer: C) Rhododendron (Laligurans)
Explanation: The Rhododendron (Laligurans) is Nepal's national flower, found abundantly in Nepal's hills and mountains.

Q5: Who unified Nepal?
A) King Tribhuvan  B) King Mahendra  C) Prithvi Narayan Shah  D) Ram Shah
Answer: C) Prithvi Narayan Shah
Explanation: Prithvi Narayan Shah, the founder of modern Nepal, unified various small kingdoms and principalities to form Nepal, starting from 1768 AD.

MATH QUESTIONS:
Q6: If a train travels 300 km in 5 hours, what is its speed?
A) 50 km/h  B) 55 km/h  C) 60 km/h  D) 65 km/h
Answer: C) 60 km/h
Explanation: Speed = Distance/Time = 300/5 = 60 km/h

Q7: What is 15% of 200?
A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 35
Answer: C) 30
Explanation: 15% of 200 = (15/100) × 200 = 15 × 2 = 30

Q8: If the simple interest on Rs. 1000 at 5% per annum for 2 years is?
A) Rs. 50  B) Rs. 100  C) Rs. 150  D) Rs. 200
Answer: B) Rs. 100
Explanation: SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (1000 × 5 × 2)/100 = 10000/100 = Rs. 100

SCIENCE QUESTIONS:
Q9: What is the chemical formula for water?
A) H₂O₂  B) HO  C) H₂O  D) H₃O
Answer: C) H₂O
Explanation: Water consists of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom, giving it the chemical formula H₂O.

Q10: What is the normal human body temperature?
A) 35°C  B) 36°C  C) 37°C  D) 38°C
Answer: C) 37°C
Explanation: The normal human body temperature is 37°C (98.6°F). Temperatures significantly above or below this indicate fever or hypothermia.

ENGLISH QUESTIONS:
Q11: Choose the correct article: "_____ Everest is the highest mountain."
A) A  B) An  C) The  D) No article
Answer: C) The
Explanation: We use 'the' with specific, unique things. Mount Everest is a unique, specific mountain, so we use 'the'.

Q12: What is the synonym of 'Abundant'?
A) Scarce  B) Plentiful  C) Limited  D) Rare
Answer: B) Plentiful
Explanation: 'Abundant' means existing in large quantities. 'Plentiful' has the same meaning. 'Scarce', 'Limited', and 'Rare' are antonyms.
